# PRODUCT.md — Frederick Akam, portfolio

**register:** brand

## Product purpose

A single page that establishes Frederick Akam as a senior software engineer with ten years
of professional experience who ships finished, production systems alone. It exists to be
linked: from a Mindrift application, from a CV, from a cold email to an agency.

It is not a creative showcase. It is a record of built systems, each with a live URL a
reader can open in one click and verify.

## Users

1. **Platform reviewers (primary, Mindrift).** Scanning to decide whether this person routes
   to the general contributor pool or the specialist engineering board. They spend under a
   minute. They need seniority, breadth, and evidence, fast.
2. **Agency and studio leads.** Considering subcontracting production work. They need proof
   he finishes things and that the finished thing looks good.
3. **Direct clients.** Evaluating whether he can be trusted with a premium build.

## The person

Frederick Akam. Kaduna, Nigeria. BSc Computer Science Education, Enugu State University of
Science and Technology. Software engineer at Efficacy Technology (Kaduna, 2016–2021) and
Seamate Maritime Integrated Services (Lagos, 2021–present). Founder of ProForge.

Working stack: Next.js, TypeScript, React, Tailwind, Supabase, Postgres, n8n, Claude API,
Python, self-hosted on Hetzner and Vercel.

## The through-line

Every serious thing he has built is an **instrument**. Something that measures a real-world
condition and reports it truthfully: property values and census data, WCAG conformance,
charging sessions and energy, ticket fraud, sponsorship pipelines. That is the spine of the
page. He builds tools that tell you the truth about something.

## Tone

Precise, factual, unhurried. States what a thing does and what it is built on, then stops.
No superlatives, no "passionate about", no adjectives doing work that a fact should do.
The confidence comes from the density of verifiable detail, not from claims.

## Anti-references

- The dark terminal developer portfolio. Monospace everything, neon on near-black, a
  hero that types itself out. This is the first reflex and it reads junior.
- The airy Swiss whitespace portfolio. Three projects floating in an ocean of margin,
  16px grey body copy, nothing to actually read.
- Identical project cards in a three-column grid with an icon and two lines each.
- Hero metrics. "10+ years. 30+ projects. 99.9% uptime."
- Any claim that cannot be clicked and checked.

## Strategic principles

1. **Every entry is verifiable.** A live URL or it does not appear.
2. **Density over air.** The reader is scanning for evidence. Give them a lot of it,
   well organised, rather than a little of it beautifully spaced.
3. **The work carries the design.** Real screenshots and real product names, not
   abstract illustration.
4. **Loads instantly anywhere.** Read on Nigerian mobile data and on a reviewer's laptop.
   No framework, no build step, no external requests.
